Questions & Answers

Straight answers about what we make, how we quote, and how projects move from design to production.

What does Seljan manufacture?
We produce metal and plastic parts. Metals include laser cutting, stamping, roll forming, fabrication, welding, powder coating, and tool & die. Plastics include rotational molding, injection molding, and blow molding — with molds built in-house.
Where are you located?
Our campus is at 100 S CP Avenue, Lake Mills, WI 53551. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 7:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. The plant operates 24/6.
Can you take a project from idea to finished part?
Yes. We typically start with a 3D SolidWorks model (or a 3D print / urethane prototype). After design approval, our mold and tooling departments build production tools. In many cases new tooling is ready for production in about 4–5 weeks.
What is rotational molding best for?
Rotomolding excels at large, hollow, durable parts in varied shapes, sizes, and colors. We run multiple rotomolding cells and robotic finishing so parts come off the line production-ready.
What metals equipment do you run?
Highlights include Amada fiber and CO₂ lasers, turret punch and combo laser systems, press brakes up to 750 tons, OBI presses to 165 tons, CNC machining centers, roll forming lines, robotic welding, and powder coat. We also carry substantial steel inventory and overhead crane capacity for staging and shipping.
Do you build your own molds and dies?
Yes. Seljan began as a tool & die shop. Our toolmakers build stamping dies, jigs, weld fixtures, and machined rotomolds — which keeps quality, lead time, and cost under our control.
What information do you need for a quote?
Send drawings or models (STEP / SolidWorks preferred), material specs, estimated annual volume, finish requirements, and any packaging or shipping needs. Call 920-648-2406 or email sales@seljan.com — or use our contact form.
Do you offer short runs and high-volume production?
Both. Injection molding supports short-run and high-volume work across eleven presses. Metals cells are set up for production scale, long parts, and inventory staging when you need scheduled releases.
Do you have a minimum order?
There is no single company-wide minimum. Quantity depends on the process — injection molding can run short jobs as well as high-volume work; rotomolding and metals are quoted to the part, tooling, and annual volume you need, including scheduled releases from staged inventory. Put the quantity on the quote and we will recommend the practical path.
What materials can you work with?
Metals cover sheet and plate from our steel inventory, stainless fabrications such as tank shells, and powder-coat color finishes. Plastics cover rotational, injection, and blow molding in specified resins, with custom colors and textures. Send material specs with the quote — or ask sales to recommend options for outdoor, industrial, or process-equipment use.
What are typical lead times?
New in-house tooling is often production-ready in about 4–5 weeks after design approval. Production after that depends on process, quantity, and whether tools already exist. The plant runs 24/6, and we can stage inventory for scheduled releases. Sales will put dates on the quote rather than a one-size estimate.
Do you ship, and do you offer trucking?
Yes. Finished parts leave from our Lake Mills campus. We have warehouse space and overhead cranes up to 20 tons to stage, pack, and ship — including freight, customer pickup, and trucking for many jobs. Include packaging and shipping needs on the quote and we will plan the move with you.
Can I tour the facility?
Facility visits can be arranged for qualified projects. Contact sales to schedule a time that works with plant operations.
