How We Work

  1. Step 1

    Consultation & Design

    We review your specs, volumes, and materials, then design for manufacturability in plastics, metals, or both.

  2. Step 2

    Prototyping

    In-house tooling and sample parts let you validate fit, finish, and function before a production run.

  3. Step 3

    Mass Production

    Rotational molding, injection molding, and metal fabrication cells scale with your order — plant hours 24/6.

  4. Step 4

    Logistics & Delivery

    Finishing, staging, and shipping leave from our Lake Mills campus so parts arrive ready to use.
