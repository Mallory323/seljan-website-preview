Injection molding

High volume & short run

Eleven presses from 22 ton to 400 ton for custom and proprietary parts — moving from SolidWorks models, 3D prints, or urethane prototypes into production tooling quickly.

Overview

Precision plastics at production scale

Injection molding is Seljan’s path for detailed plastic components where dimensional control and repeatability matter. Whether you need a pilot lot or ongoing volume, we stay true to our job-shop roots: customers and ownership of the project come first.

  • 11 presses (22–400 ton)

    Clamp tonnage is the force that holds the mold closed during injection. A range from 22 to 400 tons lets us run small precision parts up through larger components without forcing every job onto the wrong machine.

  • Shot sizes to 27 oz

    “Shot size” is how much plastic the machine can inject in one cycle. Capacity up to 27 oz supports a wide band of part weights — from compact components to larger molded pieces.

  • Custom & proprietary parts

    We mold customer-owned designs and Seljan proprietary products alike. Tooling and process are tailored to your geometry, resin, and cosmetic or structural requirements.

  • Short-run flexibility

    Not every program needs millions of pieces. Our shop supports lower-volume and pilot runs so you can launch, test markets, or bridge demand without oversized commitments.

  • High-volume capacity

    When demand ramps, multiple presses and experienced setup teams keep cycles consistent. High volume favors stable tooling, documented processes, and scheduled preventive maintenance.

  • Design-for-moldability support

    We help refine part designs for injection — draft angles, wall thickness, gate locations, and knit lines — so tools fill cleanly and parts eject reliably. Better DFM means fewer tooling revisions later.
