Recreation & Promotions

C3 Custom Coolers

C3 started in 2014 with a simple idea: move a heavy case of drinks the easy way — and have fun doing it. Every cooler is molded and foamed in Lake Mills.

The first C3 Rover went through round after round of plant-floor testing before it became the remote-control cooler people still ask for: team colors, headlights, speakers, and a real insulated box. From there the line grew into rideable coolers, floating coolers, and a custom studio that builds promotional pieces for other companies. C3 does not treat these as novelty toys first. They are thick-wall rotomolded coolers that happen to roll, float, or drive — designed, tooled, and run in the same Wisconsin factory that molds Seljan’s other plastic products.

The lineup

  • RC Rover

    The flagship remote-control cooler. Three speeds, LED headlights, Bluetooth speakers, and USB charging, with room for 24–30 cans plus ice. Body styles include Rover, pickup, delivery truck, and Zamboni — including officially licensed team graphics.

  • C3 Rider

    A cooler you sit on and drive. About 10 mph, a 300 lb capacity, roughly four hours of run time, foldable foot pegs, easy-access lid, and dry storage for gear.

  • Cruiser Craft

    A 30-can floating cooler with built-in wheels to the shoreline, a pull handle, and four cup holders so drinks stay put while you drift.

  • Transporter

    Remote-control hauler for coolers, firewood, beach gear, or yard work. About 125 lb capacity, three speeds, and onboard Bluetooth speakers.

  • Nordic 44 Quart

    A heavy-duty chest cooler with 2-inch foam walls, rope handles, flexible latches, a drain plug, and steel bottle openers. Rated to hold ice up to 10 days, and sized to ride on the Transporter.

  • Custom & promotional

    In-house designers build unique shapes, colors, and graphics for other companies — including 3D renderings and molds from Seljan’s tooling group. Many of those pieces are not sold to the public.

At a glance

  • Founded in 2014 as a Seljan division
  • Designed, molded, and assembled in Lake Mills
  • Rotationally molded walls with high-density foam
  • In-house graphics, decals, and custom tooling

Commercial Receptacles

Kolorcans

Kolorcans began as a hometown fix: rusted 55-gallon drums at a Lake Mills football field. The first cans donated to the high school are still in service.

Seljan started molding trash receptacles for other customers after adding rotational plastics in 1999. The Kolorcans name came later, after the company built 20 blue-and-yellow cans for the local school — then heard from a barber how much better the field looked. The team redesigned the can instead of copying a steel drum: recessed handles, an all-plastic dome top with no springs or hinges, and a door that closes on its own. Liners with handles followed so bags were not the only way to empty a can. Molds still come from Seljan’s in-house tool room, which is why custom sizes, weights, and lid styles stay practical. Today Kolorcans show up at universities, ballparks, beaches, hotels, malls, airports, and plants — anywhere a receptacle has to take weather and traffic.

What we mold

  • Classic & Signature series

    Round and square receptacles from about 32 to 55 gallons. Classic is the workhorse; Signature adds a slatted look for campuses and public spaces.

  • Lids that match the job

    Dome tops, funnel tops, flat tops, 2-way openings, and hole sizes for bottles or trash — including bug-barrier options. The original all-plastic dome was built without springs or hinges.

  • Liners & assemblies

    Removable liners with handles, plus complete assemblies that ship with receptacle, liner, and lid so parks and facilities can put them to work immediately.

  • Planters & stations

    Architectural planters and accessories such as hand-sanitizer stations — same fade-resistant resins as the cans, made for outdoor public spaces.

At a glance

  • Fade-resistant resins for sun, salt, and Wisconsin winters
  • Eight solid colors plus five granite finishes
  • Recessed handles and self-closing dome doors
  • Custom weights and sizes for windy coastal sites

Mach 2 Tires

Mach 2 is the original no-flat center-pivot irrigation tire, made here since 1996 from recyclable polyethylene with a UV-20+ package.

Stuck pivots and flat rubber tires during irrigation season cost crops. Mach 2 was developed as a plastic pivot tire that does not go flat, bolts onto an existing rim in two halves, and keeps sprinklers moving. The design is patented (U.S. Patent No. 9,381,773 B2). A Dow resin blend with UV inhibitor is rotomolded for crack resistance, chemical and fertilizer resistance, and long service in the sun. Tread options cover clay, mud, rolling ground, and loose sand. The company still manufactures in Lake Mills and backs the tires with a prorated 10-year warranty.

Tread designed for the field

  • Trac 1

    The aggressive tread for sticky clay, mud, and hills — used where towers get stuck and extra bite matters.

  • Trac 2

    The everyday replacement for traditional rubber pivot tires. Floats similarly in most soils on flat or rolling ground.

  • Trac 3

    Extra flotation for loose, sandy soils and flatter ground. A common choice for sod farms and hay fields.

At a glance

  • Original no-flat pivot tire since 1996
  • 100% recyclable polyethylene, UV-20+
  • Two-piece install on existing rims, any tower mix
  • Prorated 10-year warranty from Lake Mills

Cremation Products

F.H. Noble

F.H. Noble makes patented double-wall polymer urn vaults — built to protect a resting place through seasons, soil, and groundwater.

The company treats this as a product families choose once. Vaults use a patented double-wall polymer construction meant to handle ground pressure year-round and resist acid or alkaline groundwater. They are built to exceed CCIS 210.6 interment-container standards. Covers overlap, seal with silicone, and fasten at the corners with bolts. Each vault ships with a polished solid-brass engravable name plate, brass hex bolts, sealant, and appliqué plates. Colors and memorial plates are specified so funeral homes and distributors can match family wishes. Wholesale pricing is available for distributors.
