Our story

About Seljan

A family-owned manufacturer rooted in tool & die — expanded into plastics, robotics, and full-scale metal fabrication in Lake Mills, Wisconsin.

Since 1967

Built on tooling. Grown on trust.

Seljan Company began dealing exclusively in metal manufacturing. Through the 1980s and 1990s we built thousands of molds for the largest rotomolders in the United States. That experience led us to invest in our own rotational molding machines — and a plastics division that now includes injection and blow molding.

Today we manufacture over 1,000 different plastic parts and 400+ metal part numbers from our campus at 100 S CP Avenue — the expansive former SPX/APV facility known locally as "Big Blue."

Family owned

Lake Mills through and through

Seljan has been locally family owned since 1967. We are not a branch of a larger corporation — ownership still sits in Lake Mills, and the people making the calls live with the work.

Most of our management team is from this town. That independence lets us think in decades instead of quarters: we stay with customers, invest in the plant, and treat every part as something we will still be proud of years from now. Our colors are blue and yellow — the same spirit that shows up in how we build parts and support local work.
