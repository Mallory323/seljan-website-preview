Overview

Tooling that starts production

Seljan began as a tool & die shop. That craft still sits at the center of what we do: well over a century of combined journeyman experience designing and building tools that let production run accurately and on schedule — often production-ready in 4–5 weeks.

  • Stamping dies

    Hardened tool sets mounted in presses that blank, pierce, bend, or form sheet metal into finished shapes. Quality dies deliver dimensional accuracy and long life across production runs.

  • Jigs & weld fixtures

    Custom holding devices that locate parts in the correct position for drilling, assembly, or welding. Fixtures improve repeatability, reduce rework, and make robotic or manual welding more consistent.

  • Machined rotomolds

    Precision-machined molds used in rotational molding. High-quality mold surfaces and geometry help produce injection-like part quality at rotomold economics — a Seljan specialty from our mold-building roots.

  • SolidWorks design support

    3D CAD modeling used to design parts and tooling before metal is cut. SolidWorks lets us validate fit, draft, wall thickness, and tooling strategy early — reducing surprises once tools are built.

  • Prototype tooling

    Faster or simplified tools used to prove a design before full production tooling. Helps confirm form, fit, and function while controlling cost and lead time during development.

  • Production-ready molds

    Fully engineered molds built for sustained manufacturing — cooling, vents, parting lines, and durability considered so parts can run day after day with stable quality.
