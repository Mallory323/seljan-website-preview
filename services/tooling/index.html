Where Seljan Began

Tool & Die

Journeyman toolmakers building stamping dies, weld fixtures, and precision rotomolds — the foundation that still powers our metals and plastics production today.

Overview

Tooling That Starts Production

Seljan began as a tool & die shop. That craft still sits at the center of what we do: well over a century of combined journeyman experience designing and building tools that let production run accurately and on schedule — often production-ready in 4–5 weeks.

Dies & Fixtures

Hardened stamping dies blank, pierce, bend, or form sheet metal with the accuracy a production run needs. Jigs and weld fixtures locate parts for drilling, assembly, or welding so the next operation stays repeatable — including robotic weld paths.

  • Stamping Dies
  • Jigs & Weld Fixtures

Molds

Precision-machined rotomolds give hollow plastic parts injection-like quality at rotomold economics. Production-ready molds are built for cooling, vents, and parting lines so parts can run day after day — often ready in 4–5 weeks after design approval.

  • Machined Rotomolds
  • Production-Ready Molds

Design & Prototypes

SolidWorks modeling checks fit, draft, wall thickness, and tooling strategy before metal is cut. Prototype tools then prove form, fit, and function so a full production tool is not the first time the design is tested.

  • SolidWorks Design Support
  • Prototype Tooling
