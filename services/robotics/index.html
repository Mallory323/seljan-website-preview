Automation

Robotics

Robotic welding and finishing cells keep weld paths, trim, drill, and rout work consistent — so metal and plastic parts leave the line production-ready.

Overview

Weld. Finish. Repeat.

Robotics sits across metals and plastics, not in a silo. Fixtures built in tool & die hold the work; robots then run the same path on every piece — including 24/6 production.

Robotic Welding

Robotic welding adds consistency and speed for repeat weld paths on higher-volume assemblies. TIG and MIG still cover the joints that need a hand process. Automated seam welding joins long tank seams with uniform quality and less distortion than a long hand weld.

  • Robotic Weld Paths
  • TIG, MIG & Robotic Welding
  • Automated Tank Seam Welding

Robotic Finishing

After molding, robotic cells trim, drill, and rout plastic parts with consistent paths and cycle times. That cuts variation from manual secondary ops and sends parts off the line production-ready.

  • Robotic Finishing Cells
  • Trim, Drill & Rout
  • Production-Ready Parts

Repeatable Cells

Jigs and weld fixtures locate parts so robotic paths stay accurate from the first piece to the last. Independent production cells let welding and finishing run with the rest of the plant — including 24/6 schedules.

  • In-House Fixtures
  • Repeatable Weld & Trim Paths
  • 24/6 Production
