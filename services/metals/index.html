Metals Department

Metal Manufacturing

Cutting, forming, welding, and finishing — with tooling built in-house — for long parts, production volume, and staged inventory.

Overview

Cut. Form. Finish.

The metals department pairs in-house tooling with production cells built for real schedules. We carry substantial steel inventory, run overhead cranes up to 20 tons, and keep space to store and ship as you need it.

Cutting

Fiber and CO₂ lasers cut sheet and plate with clean edges. Turret punch and combo laser systems add holes, louvers, and nested profiles in one setup. CNC machining centers mill pockets, holes, and finished surfaces when a part needs tighter geometry than a nest can give.

  • Fiber & CO₂ Laser Cutting
  • Turret Punch & Combo Laser
  • CNC Machining Centers

Forming

Press brakes up to 750 tons bend thick material and long parts. OBI presses to 165 tons stamp and form with dies. Roll forming lines at 2" and 3" produce continuous profiles that would be hard to brake piece by piece. Stamping dies are built in-house by the tool & die shop.

  • Press Brakes Up To 750 Tons
  • OBI Presses To 165 Tons
  • Metal Stamping
  • Roll Forming (2" & 3" Lines)

Welding & Finishing

TIG, MIG, and robotic welding join assemblies with the process that fits the joint. Automated seam welding handles long tank seams with uniform quality. Powder coating then adds a durable, even finish — corrosion resistance and color without solvent paint.

  • TIG, MIG & Robotic Welding
  • Automated Tank Seam Welding
  • Powder Coating
