Plastics Department

Plastics Manufacturing

Rotational molding, injection molding, and robotic finishing — with molds built in-house — for strong, durable plastic parts in many shapes, sizes, and colors.

Overview

Mold. Finish. Ship.

The plastics department grew out of decades of mold-making. We tool on-site, mold the part, then finish it so it ships production-ready — rotational, injection, and blow work under one roof.

Rotational Molding

Powdered plastic coats a rotating mold, then cools into a seamless hollow part. That is how we make large tanks, housings, bins, and material-handling products that would be costly as a single injection molding. Nine cells run 24/6. Blow molding covers bottles and similar hollow containers.

  • 9 Rotomolding Work Cells
  • Large Hollow Parts
  • Material Handling Products
  • Blow Molding

Injection Molding

Molten plastic is injected into a closed mold for detailed parts that need tight dimensions — custom or proprietary, short run or high volume. Eleven presses from 22 to 400 ton, with shot sizes to 27 oz. Urethane casts and 3D prints prove a design before production tools.

  • 11 Presses (22–400 Ton)
  • Shot Sizes To 27 Oz
  • Custom & Proprietary Parts
  • Short-Run And High-Volume
  • Urethane Prototypes

Finishing

After molding, robotic cells trim, drill, and rout so parts leave the cell production-ready. Colors, textures, and surface finishes are specified with the resin and the mold — grip, matte, gloss, or a matched brand color.

  • Robotic Finishing Cells
  • Trim, Drill & Rout
  • Custom Colors & Textures
